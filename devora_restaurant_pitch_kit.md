## 🗣️ Reply Handling Script

---

### ✅ They say: "Haan, batao" / "Tell me more" / "Interested hoon"

> JazakAllah! 🙏 Toh main aap ke saath ek 15-minute screen demo schedule karta hoon — main software live dikhata hoon:
> billing, daily report, inventory tracking, aur AI suggestions.
>
> Aap ke liye kab acha hai — **kal subah** ya **shaam ko**?

*(Once they give a time, confirm it and send a reminder 1 hour before.)*

---

### 💰 They say: "Kitna charge hai?" / "Price kya hai?"

> Bilkul, price ki baat zaroor karenge — lekin pehle aap ko live demo dekhna chahiye taake aap judge kar saken ke Devora actually aap ke restaurant ke liye worth it hai ya nahi.
>
> Demo bilkul free hai, 15 minute se zyada nahi lagenge. Uske baad pricing clearly bata deta hoon. Deal?

*(This keeps price from killing the conversation before they see the value.)*

---

### ❌ They say: "Nahi chahiye" / "Interested nahi"

> Koi baat nahi, respect! 🙏
>
> Ek cheez pooch sakta hoon — abhi billing aur daily report ke liye kya use kar rahe hain? Qalam aur register, ya koi aur system?

*(This is a discovery question. If they say "register/manual" — that's your opening to come back later. If they have software already, note it as a competitor and update the CRM.)*

---

### 🤔 They say: "Baad mein baat karte hain" / "Busy hoon"

> Bilkul, aap ka waqt samajh aata hai! Main ek hafta baad dobara message kar leta hoon. 😊

*(Set a CRM reminder for +7 days and follow up once more.)*
